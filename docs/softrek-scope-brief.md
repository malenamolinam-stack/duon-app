Please re-estimate the MVP against the revised scope below. Give each item its own line so the options can be compared.

## Included in v1

- Bilingual web app (PWA) in Spanish and English, with full parity on every screen.
- Profiles for the person and their dog. Minimal onboarding: dog's name, size, and age; person's name and fitness level.
- Rules-based routine plans, generated from fixed rules that a veterinary or canine-exercise reviewer will approve.
- Activity history with manual logging (duration, distance, notes).
- Two ratings after each session: the person's effort (1–5) and the dog's comfort (1–5). A low comfort rating automatically steps the next plan down one level.
- Leash guide shown before the first outing.
- Live weather from a paid commercial licence, with a confirmation step when data is unavailable.
- Email magic-link sign-in.
- Data kept while the account is active plus 12 months, and deleted on request.
- Internal, team-only research dashboard for reviewing responses.

## Removed from v1 (compared with the prototype)

- Rewards and extra preferences.
- Simulated map, distance, and pace.
