# Questions for the veterinary or canine-exercise reviewer

These must be answered before any real user sees a plan. Until then, the thresholds and progressions in `src/engine/` stay unused.

1. **Heat limits.** At what temperature and humidity should sessions be blocked, shortened, or allowed with a caution? Should the thresholds differ by coat type, age, or breed?
2. **Session length.** What maximum duration and intensity are safe by dog size and age? What about puppies, senior dogs, and brachycephalic breeds?
3. **Progression.** How fast should a dog move up a level, and how many sessions per week is a sensible maximum?
4. **Rest days.** When should the app recommend a rest day, and for how long?
5. **Stop signals.** Which signs during or after a walk, jog, or run should tell the person to stop?
6. **Step-down rule.** Is a single one-level step-down after a low comfort rating enough, or should repeated low ratings trigger a longer rest?
7. **Health questions.** Which conditions (joint, heart, respiratory) should we ask about in a future release, and what should the app do when the answer is yes?
8. **Person's fitness.** What minimum fitness level should be required to start a jogging or running routine?
9. **Breed list.** The V17 prototype flags breeds that need a respiratory precaution. Is that list correct, and what should the app do for each flagged breed?
10. **Leash guide.** Does the leash guide (shown before the first outing) need any safety content from you before it ships?

## Open items for the reviewer

- Name and credentials of the reviewer, and whether the review is documented in writing.
- Whether the reviewer's answers should be published with the app.
