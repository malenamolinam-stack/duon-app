### Texto para publicar en Upwork (en inglés)
**Title:** Veterinarian (canine sports medicine/rehab preferred) to review exercise safety rules for a dog + owner jogging app

**Description:**
We are building DU·ON, a mobile web app that helps beginners start walking, jogging and running together with their dog, with gradual routines. Before any real user sees a plan, we need a licensed veterinarian to review our safety rules and answer 10 written questions: heat and humidity limits, session length by size and age, progression speed, rest days, stop signals, step-down after low comfort ratings, health screening questions, minimum owner fitness, brachycephalic breed list, and the leash safety guide.

Our first users live in Mérida, Mexico (hot and humid most of the year).

Deliverable: written answers to the 10 questions (we send them on hire), with sources where possible.
Requirements: DVM; canine rehabilitation or sports medicine certification (CCRT, CCRP or ACVSMR) strongly preferred; experience reviewing pet health content.
Please include your credentials and a fixed price.
